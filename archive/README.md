# Preserved design and provenance records

This directory is not needed to build or submit the chapter.

- `drafts/`: earlier cover studies, editable scenes and approval records.
- `output/`: earlier design-review documents and PDFs.
- `photographic-portraits/`: superseded photographs/PDF portrait sources and
  their original `portraits.md` provenance and permission notes. These images
  are not included in the chapter submission package.
- `pre-consolidation/`: local, untracked PDF of the reviewed version before
  source consolidation; it can also be rebuilt from Git revision `46578db`.

The original slide sources and their shared `fig/` assets have not been moved
or modified. Superseded chapter TeX fragments are recoverable from Git; their
complete active contents have been incorporated into the one chapter source.
Relative paths in old design notes refer to their original repository layout.

Changing a photograph into a sketch does not in itself establish permission
to reproduce a derivative work. The new illustrations are labelled as modern
AI-assisted interpretations, not archival images; this archive preserves the
earlier rights records rather than treating replacement as legal clearance.
