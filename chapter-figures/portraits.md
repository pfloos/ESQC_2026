# Historical portraits in the chapter

Sources and reuse statements checked on 16 October 2026. These are historical
photographs, not generated portraits. The downloaded Commons crops are used
without further pixel changes; LaTeX only scales them. Each box prints the
photographer, source, crop credit, and license or public-domain notice.

## Leon Brillouin

- Local asset: `portrait-brillouin.jpg` (170 x 238 pixels).
- Photographer: Benjamin Couprie, Institut International de Physique Solvay;
  1927 Solvay conference. Existing crop by Commons contributor Setreset.
- [Source and public-domain record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:L%C3%A9on_Brillouin_Solvay_conference_1927.jpg),
  revision 1207225405.
- Commons identifies the photograph as public domain, from publication in
  Belgium more than 70 years ago. The original was published in 1927.

## Selection limits

The Commons Slater crop found during this pass describes its identification
as only "likely". It was not used as an authenticated portrait. The current
named theorem/principle boxes therefore feature Pauli, Brillouin, and Koopmans;
the unnamed variational-bound and Gaussian-product boxes need no portrait.

## Biography additions (17 October 2026)

The eleven short biographies use institutional profiles, archival descriptions,
and scholarly memoirs linked in the boxes. They are original summaries, not
quotations. Portraits are unnumbered inline historical illustrations. Existing
Pauli, Brillouin, and Koopmans assets above are reused without pixel changes.
New assets below were downloaded as supplied; any crop is performed by LaTeX
for display only, leaving the downloaded file intact.

### Douglas Rayner Hartree

- Asset: `portrait-hartree.jpg`, 351 x 424 pixels.
- Unknown photographer, approximately 1940.
- [Commons source and public-domain statement](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Douglas_hartree.jpg),

- [Original image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/ae/Douglas_hartree.jpg).
- Biography: [MacTutor, University of St Andrews](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Hartree/).

### Clemens C. J. Roothaan

- Asset: `portrait-roothaan.jpg`, 725 x 1000 pixels; unknown photographer/date.
- [University of Chicago Photographic Archive, apf1-12856](https://photoarchive.lib.uchicago.edu/db.xqy?one=apf1-12856.xml),
  Series I: Individuals and Groups, not the Chicago Maroon collection.
- [Original image](https://pi.lib.uchicago.edu/1001/dig/photoarchive/apf1-12856/r).
- Required credit printed in the box: University of Chicago Photographic
  Archive, apf1-12856, Hanna Holborn Gray Special Collections Research Center,
  University of Chicago Library.
- [Current archive policy](https://photoarchive.lib.uchicago.edu/rights.html)
  permits educational and scholarly use with credit. This is **not a Creative
  Commons license**. Commercial publication requires Library permission.
- The obit portrait apf7-01109 was deliberately excluded: it belongs to the
  Chicago Maroon and requires separate permission under the current policy.
- Biography: [University of Chicago obituary](https://emeritifaculty.uchicago.edu/news-announcements/article/clemens-c.j.-roothaan-1918-2019).

### John Clarke Slater

- Asset: `slater-mulliken-1967.jpg`, 1000 x 801 pixels, 600 dpi.
- Larry C. Huston, Sanibel Island, Florida, 1967.
- [University of Chicago Photographic Archive, apf1-01019](https://photoarchive.lib.uchicago.edu/db.xqy?one=apf1-01019.xml).
  Its description identifies Mulliken on the left and Slater on the right.
  The chapter displays a right-hand detail following that archival identification;
  this is not an independently inferred facial identification.
- [Original image](https://pi.lib.uchicago.edu/1001/dig/photoarchive/apf1-01019/r).
- Required credit printed in the box: University of Chicago Photographic
  Archive, apf1-01019, Hanna Holborn Gray Special Collections Research Center,
  University of Chicago Library; photographer also credited.
- [Educational/scholarly use permitted with credit](https://photoarchive.lib.uchicago.edu/rights.html);
  commercial publication requires Library permission. No CC license claimed.
- Biography: [Philip M. Morse, NAS memoir](https://www.nasonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/slater-john.pdf).
- The ambiguously identified Commons Slater crop discussed above remains unused.
